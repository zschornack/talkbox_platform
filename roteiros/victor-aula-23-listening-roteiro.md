# Victor · aula 23 · roteiro do Listening (só para o professor)

Leia **uma vez só**, em velocidade normal. Antes, dê 30 segundos para ele ler as perguntas na tela.
As armadilhas (o falante muda a informação) estão marcadas com ⚠️ e são de propósito, como na prova de verdade.

---

## Part 1 · City Cycles (perguntas 1–10)

**Shop assistant:** Good morning, City Cycles, Emma speaking. How can I help you?

**Customer:** Hi, I'd like to rent a bike for a few days, please.

**Shop assistant:** Of course. Can I take your name first?

**Customer:** Yes, it's Daniel Hendricks.

**Shop assistant:** Could you spell your surname for me?

**Customer:** Sure. H-E-N-D-R-I-C-K-S. *(1)*

**Shop assistant:** Thank you. And a contact number?

**Customer:** It's 07945… 218… 336. *(2)*

**Shop assistant:** Lovely. When would you like the bike from?

**Customer:** From Friday the thirteenth… oh, no, sorry, I arrive on Friday night, so from Saturday the fourteenth of June. *(3 · ⚠️ o 13 é armadilha)*

**Shop assistant:** No problem. What kind of bike are you looking for? We have mountain bikes, road bikes and electric bikes.

**Customer:** I was thinking of a mountain bike, but the city is quite hilly, isn't it? I think I'll go for an electric bike. *(4 · ⚠️ mountain bike é armadilha)*

**Shop assistant:** Good choice. And for how many days?

**Customer:** Four days… actually, no, I'm leaving on Tuesday morning, so just three. *(5 · ⚠️)*

**Shop assistant:** The electric bikes are twenty-two pounds a day, but there's a weekend offer at the moment, so it's eighteen pounds a day. *(6 · ⚠️ 22 é armadilha)*

**Customer:** Great. Can I also get a helmet?

**Shop assistant:** Helmets are free with every rental. Do you need anything else?

**Customer:** Yes, I'm travelling with my daughter, she's four, so I'll need a child seat. *(7)*

**Shop assistant:** That's fine. What time would you like to collect the bike on Saturday? We open at eight.

**Customer:** Is half past eight OK? *(8)*

**Shop assistant:** Perfect. We're at 42 Marlowe Road. That's M-A-R-L-O-W-E. *(9)*

**Customer:** Got it. And do I need to pay anything now?

**Shop assistant:** Just a deposit. We don't accept cash for deposits, so it'll have to be by credit card. *(10 · ⚠️ cash é armadilha)*

**Customer:** No problem. Thanks very much.

**Respostas:** 1 Hendricks · 2 07945 218 336 · 3 14th June · 4 electric bike · 5 three · 6 18 · 7 child seat · 8 8.30 · 9 Marlowe Road · 10 credit card

## Part 2 · The Riverside Cycle Path (perguntas 11–15)

Good evening, everyone, and thank you for coming to this information session about the new Riverside Cycle Path.

The path follows the river from the old railway station to the university. Many people think it's twenty kilometres long because of the signs at the entrance, but those include the walking trails. The cycle path itself is twelve kilometres long. *(11 · B)*

A lot of people have asked who paid for it. The city council and several local businesses helped with smaller costs, but most of the money came from a national fund for green transport. *(12 · C)*

We've been counting cyclists since the path opened. At weekends, it's busiest in the afternoon, of course. But on weekdays, the busiest time is actually early in the morning, between seven and nine, when people are cycling to work. Lunchtime is surprisingly quiet. *(13 · A)*

Now, about dogs. We know many of you walk your dogs by the river. Dogs are welcome on the whole path, but they must be kept on a lead at all times, for the safety of cyclists. *(14 · C · ⚠️ "whole path" é armadilha)*

Finally, some good news. Many people asked for a café, and we're still discussing that. The lights were installed last week. And next month, we're adding five bike repair points, with free tools and air pumps for your tyres. *(15 · C)*

Thank you. Are there any questions?

**Respostas:** 11 B · 12 C · 13 A · 14 C · 15 C
